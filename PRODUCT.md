# Geode

Music you can see.

## What Geode is

Geode is an Android phone app that plays your music and turns it into visuals. Every song comes alive as a moving picture that reacts to the sound: flowing particles, liquids, living cell patterns, fractals, sound-wave physics, and classic MilkDrop visuals. You choose the look, shape it until it is yours, play with it by touch, and keep it: record it live, or render a song or a whole playlist into a video to edit, save or share.

People download Geode for the music visuals. It also works as a complete music player when you only want to listen.

It is for anyone who loves music and visuals, not a specialist tool. It is easy to start with, with depth for people who want to dig in. Geode works fully offline: there is no account and no sign-in. The internet is only needed to share videos.

## The main sections

The app has a home screen and these main sections:

- **Music Library**: your music.
- **Player**: the song that is playing, with the visual behind it.
- **Visuals**: choose a look, customize it, and watch it live.
- **Studio**: turn music and visuals into video, and edit videos.
- **Settings**: your preferences, safety options and help.

A navigation bar at the bottom moves between the sections. Studio can be hidden in Settings by people who only come to listen.

## Home screen

The home screen is the first screen you see every time you open Geode. It is a hub. It shows artwork of the last visual you played and the last song, ready to resume, and it shows what you can do in the app. You can change what the home screen shows in Settings, for example shortcuts, Presets or your last played.

## Music Library

A normal music library, with tabs for **Tracks, Albums, Artists, Folders and Playlists**. Each track shows its artwork, title, artist, album and length, and a menu with more actions. You can search titles, artists and albums, and sort by title, artist, album, length or date added.

Tapping a track plays it and queues everything you are looking at, so Next and Previous behave the way you expect. While a song plays, a mini player sits at the bottom or the top of the screen, whichever you choose. An **eye icon** at the top opens the visual that is currently loaded. If no visual is loaded yet, it takes you to the Visuals section to choose one.

The library is the way into the visuals: songs played here drive the visual. It is also where you pick songs and playlists to turn into videos.

### Player features

- **Playlists and queue**: your own playlists, smart playlists that fill themselves by rules, importing and exporting playlists, a play queue, favourites and listening history.
- **Listening tools**: a sleep timer that fades the music out, A-B loop to repeat part of a song, resume where you left off, and smooth transitions between songs (gapless or crossfade).
- **Sound tools**: an equalizer, crossfeed for headphones, and volume levelling so songs play at a similar loudness.
- **Lyrics**, shown alongside the song.

### Outside the app

- **Lock screen and notification**: media controls, and the music keeps playing in the background.
- **Home-screen widget**: shows what's playing, with controls.
- **Live wallpaper**: the visual as your phone's wallpaper.

## Player

The Player shows the song that is playing: its artwork, the controls and a live spectrum of the sound. Tapping the artwork goes full screen, where the visual lives.

With the visual playing behind it, the Player shows:

- At the top: the song title and artist, a heart to favourite the song, and a button to fold the controls away.
- At the bottom, on a see-through panel: a seek bar with the time played and the length, and shuffle, previous, play/pause, next and repeat.
- A **Visuals** button that jumps to the Visuals section.
- Tabs for **Now** (the controls), **Lyrics**, **Queue** (with the number of songs), and **A-B** to loop part of the song.

## Visuals

This is where the music comes to life. You come here to watch, to set a mood, and to make a look your own. It is also used for:

- **Live performance**: visualizing a musician or DJ through the microphone.
- **Content creation**: making a look to record or render into clips for social media.

The Visuals section has four tabs, **Presets, Styles, Customize and Textures**, plus a **View live** button that shows the visual full screen.

### Sound sources

The visual reacts to sound. The sound comes from:

- **Your music library**: the song that is playing.
- **The microphone**: sound in the room, such as music from a speaker, a live performance or a party. The visual reacts to the mic the same way it reacts to library music.

More sound sources may come later.

### Style Families and Styles

- **Style Family**: a group of Styles built on the same base method. Each Family moves and reacts to music in its own way. Families include Silk, Life, Mycelium, Acid, Fluid, Cymatics, Shaders and MilkDrop. New Families are added regularly.
- **Style**: one variation within a Family, with its own name, such as Halvorsen Web, Cosine Bloom or Pendulum Garden in Silk. It uses the same base as its Family but has its own look and extra touches. There are around sixty Styles.

The **Styles** tab shows the Families as tabs, each with its list of Styles. You tap a Style to load it.

### MilkDrop

MilkDrop is a classic kind of music visual with a large world of community-made presets. Geode ships a set of MilkDrop presets. In the MilkDrop Family you can **load a single .milk file** or **import a whole folder** of them, with their textures, and they appear under **Your .milk presets**.

### Presets

- **Preset**: a saved set of customization settings only. It can be used on other Styles.
- **Scene Preset**: a saved Family and Style together with every customization, a complete look in one tap.

The **Presets** tab shows the built-in presets and your own. You can:

- **Save current as…** a new preset with a name.
- Organise presets into **folders**.
- **Share** a preset as a file or as text through the share menu.
- **Paste a shared preset** or **open a preset file** that someone sent you.

### Watching

Once a Style is chosen, the visual plays. You can:

- Tap **View live** to see only the visual, full screen.
- **Drag down** to look at the whole visual behind the panels.
- Adjust a **translucency slider**, so the panels are see-through and your choices and edits show on the visual straight away.
- **Long-press** in full screen to bring up a side bar with the Macros and a red **record** button. Which gestures do what is set in Settings.

When you switch to another look, the visual changes over with a smooth transition.

When you leave the Visuals section, the visual pauses and the music keeps playing. When the next song starts, the same look continues.

### Touching the visual

On the full-screen visual, your finger is part of the picture:

- **One finger** sets a point the Style builds itself around.
- **Two fingers** set an axis.
- **Three or more** open a swirl.
- **Pinch** zooms and **twist** rotates.

Tilting the phone also moves the visual, as if gravity pulls it.

### Customize

Customization changes how the visual looks and behaves: shape, colour, motion, effects, how it reacts to the music, and how it reacts to you. It has three layers:

1. **Macros**: six big knobs, each bundling settings that belong together, for a quick and easy feel.
2. **Customization**: clear, intuitive settings that anyone can use.
3. **Advanced**: a detailed modulation matrix for people who want to go deep. It has a **patch board** for wiring parts of the sound to settings, and **modulators** (such as LFOs) that move a setting by themselves over time.

The Customize tab is split into topics: **Shape, Colour, Motion, FX and Reactions**. Each topic has groups of sliders. For example, Motion has a Movement group with Speed, Zoom, Rotation, Sway and Turbulence. On each topic you can:

- **Search every parameter** by name.
- **Randomize** that topic for a surprise, for example Randomize Motion. A **lock** on each setting keeps it fixed when you randomize.
- **Undo** and **Redo** your changes.
- **Compare A/B**: save two versions as A and B and switch between them to pick the better one.

You can save and reuse colour **palettes**.

A customization has the same effect in every Style Family. Each Family still keeps its own character in how it responds to the music.

You can keep your work by saving a **Scene Preset** or a **Preset**. Your current look is also kept automatically, even if you don't save it.

### Textures

The **Textures** tab holds the images some Styles and MilkDrop presets draw with. You can add your own.

### Recording

The record button captures the visual and the sound live, as you watch:

- It never records the app's own screens.
- It keeps recording even if you move to another screen.
- You can keep playing with Macros, touch and tilt while recording. Those changes appear in the video, so the recording is a performance.
- The recording is saved to your phone and can be opened in Studio.

By default a recording is one video with the visual and music together. In Settings you can choose to also save every control change (Macros, customizations) next to the video as separate lanes. You can edit those lanes later in Studio.

### Casting

You can send the visual to a bigger screen, such as a TV.

## Studio

Studio is where music and visuals become video. It has two parts.

### Export lounge

Here you render a video offline from the music:

- **What**: a single song, or a whole playlist. By default, what is playing.
- **Look**: by default, the current visual. You can also pick a Scene Preset.
- **Format and quality**: vertical, square or wide, and the resolution.
- **Loop**: optionally render a short video that loops seamlessly.
- **Chapters**: a long playlist video gets chapter markers, one per song.
- **Loudness**: the audio is levelled to a good loudness for sharing.

While rendering you see progress and the time left. Rendering keeps going in the background, you can keep listening to music meanwhile, and a notification tells you when the video is ready. A rendered video lands in Studio, ready to be cut, graded and sent somewhere.

A rendered video reacts to the music even better than the live visual, because the render can look ahead in the song.

There are three ways to start an export:

- **Quick Export**: renders the song that is playing, from the start, with the current visual settings.
- **From Studio**, in the export lounge.
- **From the Music Library**, from a song or playlist.

### Editing lounge

Here you edit videos: ones Geode rendered, recordings, or any other video on your phone (**Open a video…**). You can:

- Trim and cut, on a **timeline** with keyframes, curves, markers and undo.
- **Auto-cut** clips on the beat of the music.
- Join clips, with **transitions** between them.
- Change speed with **speed ramps**.
- Add text, such as the song title, artist, captions, subtitles or lyrics, timed to the music.
- Apply filters and **colour grading**.

Your work is kept as a **project** you can save and come back to.

If a recording was saved with its control lanes, you can also see those lanes, edit them, add new changes, and re-render a clean video from the song.

When you're done, you save the video to your phone or share it through Android's share menu.

## Settings

All your preferences, in tabs sorted by topic:

- **Look**: how the app looks, such as accent intensity, background dim and font colour.
- **Audio**: playback and sound tools.
- **Export**: default format, quality and save location.
- **Folders**: which folders Geode reads music from, and default folders.
- **Home screen**: what the hub shows.
- **Visualizer**: gestures, panel translucency, full-screen behaviour, and recording options such as saving control lanes.
- **Library and player**: mini-player position and playback.
- **Accessibility and safety**: the flash-safety options and reduced motion (see below).
- **Language**: the app's language.
- **Sections**: hide sections you don't use, such as Studio.
- **Help**: the tour and tutorials, available any time.
- **Diagnostics**: send a problem report, with personal data removed.
- **Import**: bring over your library and data from an older version of Geode.

## Safety and accessibility

- **Flash safety**: visuals can flash. Geode shows a warning and asks for consent before the first visual. A flash limiter and a no-flash mode protect people who are sensitive to flashing light.
- **Reduced motion**: calmer animations in the app for people who need it.
- **Readable contrast**: text stays readable for everyone.

## First-run tour

The first time you open Geode, a short tour of six cards walks through the app, one card per section:

1. **This is the Player**: your track, the controls and a live spectrum. Tap the artwork to go full screen, where the visuals live.
2. **Your music lives here**: tracks, albums, artists, folders and playlists. Tapping a track queues everything you are looking at.
3. **Sixty-odd visual styles**: pick a Style, then open Customize to change how it moves. Presets save a whole look, and Randomize on any tab is the fastest way to find one you like.
4. **Touch the visuals**: how one, two and three fingers, pinch and twist shape the visual.
5. **Studio makes videos**: record a performance as you play with the controls, then render it at any quality. You can hide this tab if you only came to listen.
6. **Everything else is in Settings**: themes, audio, playback, folders and safety. Help lives there too, and you can replay the tour any time.

Each card has Skip, Back and Next, and a "Don't show this next time" box.

## A typical session

1. **First open.** Geode asks permission to read your music and scans your library. The tour shows the main sections. You land on the home hub.
2. **Play a song.** In the Music Library you tap a song. It plays, and the mini player appears.
3. **See it.** You tap the eye icon. With no look chosen yet, the Visuals section opens.
4. **Choose a look.** In Styles you pick a Family, then a Style. The visual starts moving to the song.
5. **Make it yours.** In Customize you open Colour and Motion, randomize a few times, lock the settings you like, and compare A and B, with the panels see-through so you can watch each change happen.
6. **Keep it.** In Presets you save it with a name.
7. **Play with it.** You tap View live, pinch and twist the visual, long-press for the Macros, and hit record.
8. **Make a video.** In Studio you render the whole song as a vertical video, keep listening while it renders, then trim it, add the song title and share it.
9. **Next day.** The home screen shows yesterday's song and look, ready to resume.
10. **At a party.** You switch the visual to the microphone, go full screen, and cast it to the TV.
