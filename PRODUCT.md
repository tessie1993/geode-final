# Geode

> This is an overview of the app, for understanding the app. It is not meant to list every aspect of Geode. It gives Claude context on what we are building.

## What we are building

Geode is an Android music player that turns music into visuals. Every song becomes a moving picture that reacts to the sound. You choose the look, make it your own, and keep the moments you like as a recording or a rendered video.

People come to Geode for the visuals, but it is also a complete music player that works just as well when you are not watching. It is made for anyone who loves music and visuals: easy to start with in a minute, with plenty of depth for people who want to go further. Geode works fully offline, with no account and no sign-in. The internet is only needed to share a video.

## The shape of the app

Every visit starts on the home screen. It shows the last song and the last visual, ready to resume, and leads to the four main places in the app:

- **Music Library**: where your music lives and plays.
- **Visuals**: where the music comes to life.
- **Studio**: where music and visuals become video.
- **Settings**: where you make the app work your way.

The usual path through the app is simple. You play a song in the Music Library, tap the eye icon to see it as a visual, shape the look until you like it, and record it or send it to Studio to become a video you can save or share.

## Music Library

A full music player in its own right. It finds the music on your phone and lets you browse and search tracks, artists, albums and playlists. It has the tools people expect from a good player, such as playlists and a queue, smooth transitions between songs, a sleep timer and sound settings. Music keeps playing in the background, with a mini player in the app and controls on the lock screen. The library is also the way in to everything else: songs played here drive the visual, and any song or playlist can be sent to Studio.

## Visuals

The heart of Geode. A visual reacts to a song from your library, or to the sound in the room through the microphone. That makes Geode useful on the sofa, at a party, at a live show, or for making clips for social media.

Every look belongs to a **Style Family**, a group of visuals that move and react to music in the same way. Each Family has **Styles**, variations with their own look. New Families are added over time. Geode also includes MilkDrop, the classic music visualizer, with built-in presets and support for importing your own.

You can watch a visual full screen, see your changes on it straight away, and send it to a bigger screen such as a TV.

## Making it yours

Customization changes how a visual looks and behaves, in three layers so everyone finds their level:

1. **Macros**: six big controls for quick, satisfying changes.
2. **Customization**: clear settings anyone can understand.
3. **Advanced**: a full modulation system for people who want to go deep.

A setting means the same thing in every Family, while each Family keeps its own character. A look can be saved as a **Scene Preset** (the whole look) or a **Preset** (just the settings), and Geode remembers your current look even if you don't save it.

Moving the visual by tilting the phone, so that gravity pulls it around, is a planned feature that is not built yet.

## Recording and video

While you watch, you can record the visual and the sound together and keep playing with it while it records.

Studio turns music and visuals into finished video. In the **export lounge** Geode renders a song or a whole playlist into a video, in the format and quality you choose, working in the background while you keep listening. Because it renders from the music itself, it can react even more precisely than the live visual. In the **editing lounge** you trim, cut and finish videos with a timeline, transitions, text and colour, then save them to your phone or share them.

## Safety and care

Visuals can flash, so Geode warns you and asks for consent before the first one plays, and offers ways to limit or turn off flashing. Reduced motion and readable text keep the app comfortable for everyone. Tutorials and help are always available, and a short tour on first open introduces the main features.
